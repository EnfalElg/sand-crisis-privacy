# Privacy Policy - Sand Crisis

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile game **Sand Crisis** ("the Game").

## 1. Information We Collect

Sand Crisis does not require users to create an account, provide a name, or directly provide personal information in order to play the Game.

However, the Game uses third-party advertising and monetization services. These services may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

- Device identifiers and advertising identifiers
- IP address
- Approximate location derived from the IP address
- Device model, operating system, and language
- Application identifier and application version
- Information about advertisements shown to you
- Advertisement views, clicks, and interactions
- Technical and diagnostic information
- Certain gameplay or in-app activity related to advertising and monetization

This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, maintain services, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

## 2. Advertising

Sand Crisis uses **Unity LevelPlay** and advertising services provided by Unity and its advertising partners.

These services may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and information about interactions with advertisements.

Information may also be shared with advertising networks, advertisers, publishers, attribution providers, and other advertising technology partners for purposes such as:

- Providing advertisements
- Measuring advertising performance
- Preventing fraud
- Improving advertising services

Depending on your region and device settings, you may be provided with privacy controls or consent options regarding personalized advertising.

## 3. Rewarded Advertisements

Sand Crisis may allow players to voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements in exchange for in-game rewards.

Advertising providers may process information relating to whether an advertisement was shown, watched, completed, or interacted with in order to provide the advertisement and verify the reward.

## 4. In-App Purchases

Sand Crisis may offer optional in-app purchases.

Payments are processed by the applicable application marketplace, such as Google Play.

We do not directly receive or store your payment card or banking information.

The marketplace and relevant service providers may process information necessary to:

- Complete purchases
- Prevent fraud
- Maintain transaction records

These services process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the Game or third-party services may be used to:

- Operate and maintain the Game
- Provide advertisements
- Provide rewarded advertisements
- Measure advertising performance
- Process optional in-app purchases
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
- Diagnose technical problems
- Improve the stability and performance of the Game
- Comply with legal obligations

## 6. Third-Party Services

Sand Crisis uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies.

These may include:

- Unity Technologies
- Unity LevelPlay
- Unity Ads
- Participating advertising partners
- Google Play services related to distribution and in-app purchases

These third parties are responsible for their own data processing practices in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not directly sell personal information.

However, information may be processed or shared by advertising and technology providers as necessary to provide:

- Advertising
- Advertising measurement
- Attribution
- Fraud prevention
- Related technical services

Advertising identifiers and device-related information may be shared between advertising technology partners where permitted by applicable law and your privacy choices.

## 8. Data Retention

We do not maintain a separate user account database for Sand Crisis.

Information processed by third-party service providers is retained according to their own retention policies and legal obligations.

Information directly received from users, such as support communications, may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game.

Third-party service providers are responsible for protecting information processed through their own systems.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request:

- Access to certain personal information
- Correction of certain personal information
- Deletion of certain personal information

Advertising providers may also provide privacy controls that allow users to manage personalized advertising and certain data processing.

You may also limit or reset advertising-related identifiers through your device's privacy settings where supported.

For questions or requests regarding information controlled directly by Sand Crisis, you may contact us using the email address listed below.

For information controlled by third-party advertising providers, users may also exercise their privacy rights through the privacy tools provided by those services.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Sand Crisis does not knowingly request users to provide personal information directly through the Game.

Advertising and data-processing practices may vary depending on the user's region, age settings, and applicable laws.

Where required, appropriate advertising and privacy settings should be applied for younger users.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to:
